Open the link in a desktop browser and click **Customize widgets** to begin.

## What this mockup demonstrates

The proposed feature lets a Savi user describe a financial view, compare three designs, refine the chosen widget, and organize it on their Overview dashboard. The walkthrough demonstrates creation, design selection, refinement/undo, reopening, pinning, and reordering.

## Three-minute walkthrough

| Step | Action | Expected result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | On Overview, click **Customize widgets**, then **Create widget**. | The widget request screen opens. |
| 2 | Use the displayed example, **“Show my spending from January to March.”** Click **Generate 3 designs**. | A short loading state advances to three choices. **View designs** is also available if needed; **Cancel** returns to the request. |
| 3 | Choose **Bar chart**, **Trend line**, or **Donut chart**. | The selected chart appears in **Your widget**, with the request and selection in **History**. |
| 4 | Click the send arrow beside **Make it blue**. | The chart turns blue and History records the refinement. |
| 5 | Click **Undo last prompt**. | The chart returns to gold and the refinement disappears from History. Send the refinement again if desired. |
| 6 | Use **Change design** to try another option. | The chart type changes; the selected color and refinement history are retained. |
| 7 | Click **Add to Overview**, then click the pinned chart. | The selected widget appears on the dashboard and reopens with its design and history. Click **Done** to return. |
| 8 | Scroll **inside the phone** to the bottom of Overview. Click **Reorder widgets**, then **Move widget below Net worth**. | Net worth appears before Monthly Spending. Scroll back up to see the order; **Reset widget order** reverses it. |
| 9 | Open **Customize widgets** and choose **Remove from Overview**. Open **Customize widgets** again. | The widget is unpinned but remains available through **Open widget** during the same prototype session. |

To start over, reopen the link at the top of this page. Use full screen if the phone looks too small. If access later changes, contact the team for view access.
